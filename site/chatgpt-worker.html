HOW IT WILL WORK
  1. Connect the Satoshi Hunt Worker Control MCP app to ChatGPT.
  2. Sign in to the Satoshi Hunt account through OAuth and approve worker:read and worker:control.
  3. Ask ChatGPT commands such as “show my live puzzles” or “run the highest-ranked eligible puzzle”.
  4. ChatGPT calls the worker-scoped MCP tool; Satoshi Hunt validates the action before changing the assignment.
  5. The Satoshi Hunt browser remains open and continues the worker heartbeat/control loop.

Write/control MCP support depends on the ChatGPT plan and workspace. The connection contract is already prepared in this repository; production OAuth/MCP deployment is the next infrastructure step.
